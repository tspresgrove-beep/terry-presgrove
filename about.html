About the Author

Terry Presgrove was born in Elk City, Oklahoma, in 1949, the son of Denton and Janell Presgrove. A former combat Marine and a follower of Jesus Christ, he is the author of three books, the father of one son, and the husband of Lisa—the love of his life.

Terry served in the Vietnam War as an artillery forward observer with the 1st Battalion, 9th Marine Regiment, famously known as The Walking Dead. The battalion sustained the highest killed-in-action rate in Marine Corps history, with 749 of its Marines’ names now etched on the Vietnam Veterans Memorial.

Marked by war, loss, and the hard trials of life—including divorce and long seasons of struggle—Terry has known both profound joy and devastating sorrow. His writing reflects those lived extremes with honesty and restraint, bearing witness to the realities of combat, the enduring bond between Marines, and the sustaining presence of faith and hope amid suffering and grace.
